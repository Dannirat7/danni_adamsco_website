Accessibility

Everyone should be able to use this website.

Operation Better Together is committed to providing an accessible and welcoming online experience.

Our commitment

We work to make this website accessible to people with disabilities and aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Accessibility features in this prototype

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and controls
  • A skip-to-content link
  • Clear heading structure
  • Readable contrast and scalable text
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Plain-language content and descriptive links

Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing content, please contact Operation Better Together so an accessible format or direct assistance can be provided.

Before launch: Add the final accessibility contact email and phone number, test with keyboard-only navigation and a screen reader, and complete an automated and manual accessibility review.
